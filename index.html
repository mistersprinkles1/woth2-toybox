WOTH2 Toybox - Way of the Hunter 2 map and helper

WOTH2 Toybox is a free, unofficial fan-made map and helper for Way of the Hunter 2. It runs in your browser on PC and phone, and as an app for Windows and Linux.

  • 3D and 2D maps of New Laurentia, Kilimaya Reserve and Hearth & Hunt HQ
  • Every herd, its size and its daily routine, with drinking, eating and resting need zones
  • Rare coats, trophy stars and the fitness and age a 5 star animal needs
  • An encyclopedia of animals, weapons, ammo and skills
  • Pins and notes, with cloud save across your devices

Download the WOTH2 Toybox app for Windows (free)
